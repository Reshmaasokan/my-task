My Personal Blog

Introduction

My name is Reshma, and I am from Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu.
I did my elementary schooling near my home, where I was known as an obedient and disciplined student.
Those early years taught me the importance of hard work and dedication, which continue to guide me in my studies and everyday life.

Later, I pursued my bachelors degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at Anna University Regional Campus, Tirunelveli, where I gained both academic knowledge and practical exposure.
