Product Features

  • Power & Battery
    • 40-Hour Battery Life: Enjoy days of uninterrupted music playback or voice calls on a single charge.
    • Rapid Fast Charging: Get up to 4 hours of listening time from a quick 10-minute USB-C charge.
    • Smart Eco-Power: Auto-pause sensors automatically shut down audio when you take the headphones off to preserve energy.
  • Tech & Connectivity
    • Active Noise Cancellation (ANC):Advanced hybrid technology filters out up to 95% of low-frequency ambient background noise.
    • Bluetooth 5.3 Multipoint:Seamlessly connect and switch between two devices simultaneously without lagging.
    • Instant Voice Assistant Access:One-touch activation for Siri, Google Assistant, or Alexa.
  • Build & Ergonomics
    • Plush Cloud-Foam Cushioning:Ultra-soft, breathable earcups designed to prevent ear fatigue during long sessions.
    • Feather-Light Frame:Reinforced, lightweight materials combine structural durability with maximum comfort.
    • Foldable Travel Design: Rotating earcups collapse flat into a compact footprint for easy storage in your bag.
  • Audio Performance
    • Custom-Tuned 40mm Drivers:Delivers deep, resonant bass alongside exceptionally clear mids and crisp highs.
    • Hi-Res Audio Certified:Experience studio-grade acoustic reproduction exactly as the artist intended.
    • Quad-Microphone System:Four beamforming microphones isolate your voice from background noise for crystal-clear calls.
